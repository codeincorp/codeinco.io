Makers of Taxy

Taxy is a personal tax assistant app that keeps your taxes perpetually in progress instead of piling up at filing time. It connects to your bank and brokerage accounts, classifies every transaction with cash-flow categories and tax tags, matches transfers between your accounts, and maintains a running tax preview — so April never comes as a surprise.

Taxy has read-only access to your accounts. It never moves money, and it never sells or shares your data.
